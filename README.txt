OpenWithLove Birthday & Anniversary Catalog

Opening the website shows a welcome screen with two side-by-side collection folders: Birthdays and Anniversaries. Click a folder to open its templates. Use “Back to collections” to return.

Birthday collection: B1-B6 and Sample Birthday Surprise.
Anniversary collection: A1-A6, with A1(1) nested under A1 and A4(1)-A4(4) nested under A4.

Deploy the contents of the OpenWithLove_Birthday_Catalog folder to your existing Vercel project to update the live catalog.
